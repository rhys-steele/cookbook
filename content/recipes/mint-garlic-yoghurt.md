## Overview
Thick Greek yoghurt with raw garlic, fresh mint, lemon juice, and salt. Rest 10 minutes for flavours to meld. Drizzle on grilled meats, roast veg, or flatbreads. Activation for store-bought greek yoghurt: add extra garlic, fresh mint, and lemon zest if available.
