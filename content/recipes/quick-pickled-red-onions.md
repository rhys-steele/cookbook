## Raw Idea
Slice red onions thin, pour boiling brine (vinegar, water, sugar, salt) over them, wait 30 minutes. That's it. Bright pink, tangy, crunchy. Elevates tacos, burgers, grain bowls, sandwiches. Lasts weeks in the fridge. The easiest way to make any meal look and taste more interesting.

## Overview
The universal upgrade. Thinly sliced red onions transform in minutes when hit with hot sweet-salty brine, turning bright pink and mellowing from sharp to tangy-sweet. They add crunch, acid, color, and visual appeal to almost anything. This is the recipe you'll make weekly once you start.

## Why This Works
The hot vinegar brine softens the onions' cell walls and draws out their sharp sulfur compounds, replacing them with tangy acidity. Sugar balances the vinegar's harshness. Salt seasons and helps preserve. The anthocyanins in red onions react with the acid, creating that vibrant pink color. Quick pickling (as opposed to fermentation) means they're ready fast and stay crisp.

## Key Technique
**Thin and even slicing.** The thinner and more uniform your slices, the better and faster they pickle. Aim for 2-3mm. Too thick and they stay too crunchy and sharp. Use a mandoline if you have one - it's faster and more consistent.

## Shortcut
Buy pre-sliced red onions if your supermarket has them. Or use a mandoline to slice them in 30 seconds flat. Some stores even sell quick-pickled onions in jars, but they're expensive and not as good as fresh.

## Variations
- **Jalapeño pickled onions**: Add 1-2 sliced jalapeños to the jar for heat
- **Mexican-style**: Add dried oregano and a pinch of cumin to the brine
- **Sweet version**: Double the sugar for sweeter pickles
- **Red wine vinegar**: Swap half the white vinegar for red wine vinegar for deeper flavor
- **Spiced**: Add whole cumin seeds, coriander seeds, or chili flakes to the brine

## Storage
- **Fridge**: 2-3 weeks in an airtight jar, fully submerged in brine
- **Freezer**: Not recommended - they'll lose their crunch
- **Counter**: Use within a few hours if kept at room temp

## Make Ahead
Perfect make-ahead component. Make a jar on Sunday, use all week. They actually get better after 24 hours as flavors meld.

## Troubleshooting
- **Not pink enough?** Your onions might not be very red, or you used too much water. Try using all vinegar next time, or add a few drops of beet juice for color.
- **Too sharp/strong?** Let them pickle longer, or use more sugar in the brine, or rinse them under cold water before using.
- **Too soft?** Brine was too hot or they pickled too long. Aim for hot (not boiling) brine and check them after 30 mins.
- **Brine cloudy?** Probably just particulates from the onions - totally fine and safe.

## Uses / Pairs With
Use them on literally everything:
- **Tacos** - Especially beef birria, carnitas, fish tacos
- **Burgers and hot dogs** - Instant upgrade
- **Grain bowls** - Add brightness and crunch
- **Sandwiches** - Bánh mì, pulled pork, any sandwich
- **Eggs** - Scrambled eggs, omelettes, breakfast burritos
- **Salads** - Adds acid and texture
- **Cheese boards** - Cuts through rich cheese
- **Rice dishes** - Top fried rice, burrito bowls, poke
- **Pizza** - Yes, seriously, try it

## Additional Notes
This recipe is endlessly scalable - double or triple it if you go through them fast. The brine can be reused 2-3 times for fresher batches (just reheat and add a bit more sugar/salt). Red onions work best for color and flavor, but you can pickle any onion. This is one of those "changes your cooking" recipes that takes 10 minutes and unlocks dozens of meals. Make a jar today.
