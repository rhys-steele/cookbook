## Raw idea:

Thinly sliced red onion in a jar with red wine vinegar, sugar, salt, and warm water. Ready in 20 minutes, better after an hour. Keeps for weeks in the fridge. Adds brightness and crunch to tacos, grain bowls, sandwiches, and platters.
