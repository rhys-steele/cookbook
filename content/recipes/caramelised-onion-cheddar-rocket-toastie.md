## Overview
Sourdough, sharp cheddar, a generous spoonful of caramelised onions, and a handful of rocket stuffed in after toasting for freshness and peppery bite. Butter the outside of the bread and press in a hot pan until golden. The caramelised onions make it — sweet against the sharp cheese.
