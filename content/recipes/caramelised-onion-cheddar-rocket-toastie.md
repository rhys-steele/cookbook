## Raw Idea
Sourdough, sharp cheddar, generous spoonful of caramelised onions, handful of rocket stuffed in after toasting for freshness and peppery bite. Butter the outside, press in hot pan until golden. The caramelised onions make it — sweet against sharp cheese. Takes 10 minutes when you have the onions on hand.

## Overview
The elevated grilled cheese. Sweet caramelised onions meet sharp cheddar in golden, crispy sourdough, finished with fresh peppery rocket. It's comfort food with sophistication. Having caramelised onions in your freezer makes this a 10-minute weeknight dinner or quick lunch that feels special.

## Why This Works
Caramelised onions add sweet, jammy depth that transforms basic cheese toastie into something restaurant-quality. Sharp cheddar provides tangy counterpoint to sweet onions. Rocket (added after cooking) stays fresh and peppery, cutting through richness. Butter on the outside creates crispy, golden crust. The contrast of textures (crispy bread, melted cheese, jammy onions, fresh rocket) and flavors (sweet, sharp, peppery) makes every bite interesting.

## Key Technique
**Add rocket after cooking.** This is crucial. Rocket added before cooking = sad, wilted, bitter greens. Rocket stuffed in after = fresh, peppery bite that cuts through the richness. Open the hot sandwich, stuff it in, press back together.

## Shortcut
Use jarred caramelised onions (check ingredients - should be just onions, oil, maybe vinegar). Use pre-grated cheese. Use a toastie maker or sandwich press instead of pan. Skip the rocket if you must. Still tasty, just less special.

## Variations
- **Blue cheese version**: Mix blue cheese with cheddar for extra punch
- **Balsamic**: Drizzle balsamic reduction over onions
- **Apple**: Add thin apple slices for sweet-tart crunch
- **Mushroom**: Add sautéed mushrooms with the onions
- **Truffle**: Drizzle truffle oil on rocket before adding
- **Different cheese**: Gruyere, aged gouda, or fontina all work beautifully

## Storage
- **Fridge**: Don't store assembled - bread gets soggy. Keep components separate and assemble fresh.
- **Freezer**: Not recommended for assembled sandwich
- **Counter**: Serve immediately while hot and crispy

## Make Ahead
Have caramelised onions frozen in portions. Grate cheese ahead if desired. Assemble and cook fresh - this is a 10-minute meal, no need to prep ahead.

## Troubleshooting
- **Bread burning before cheese melts?** Heat too high. Use medium heat, be patient, or cover pan with lid.
- **Cheese not melting?** Sliced too thick, or heat too low. Grate cheese for faster melting.
- **Rocket wilting too much?** Added too early. Wait until sandwich is off heat, open it, stuff in rocket quickly.
- **Too greasy?** Too much butter, or butter not spread evenly. Use just enough to coat.
- **Falling apart?** Not enough cheese to bind, or opened it too roughly.

## Additional Notes
The quality of bread matters here - good sourdough makes this special, sad white bread makes it just okay. Sharp/mature cheddar is crucial - mild cheddar won't have enough flavor to stand up to the sweet onions. If you don't have caramelised onions, this dish isn't worth making - that's the star ingredient. Make a batch, freeze portions, unlock this sandwich whenever. Room temperature butter is easier to spread evenly without tearing bread. If using a toastie maker, reduce butter slightly as they need less fat. This is the kind of simple, perfect lunch that makes you feel like you have your life together. Cost is about £2-3 per sandwich. Scales easily for multiple sandwiches.
