## Overview
This is a “weeknight cheat” pho ga built off my homemade chicken stock base, with a traditional pho flavour profile and spice set. Using stock massively boosts depth and body, but it will reduce the crystal-clear broth look — accept the trade-off, skim well, and keep the aromatics clean. Char onion and ginger, toast the whole spices, then combine with stock and fish sauce/sugar to hit that classic pho balance. Garnish heavily: bean sprouts, fresh chilli, coriander, Thai basil, mint, chilli oil, brown onion, and (my extra) shredded purple cabbage for crunch and colour — surprisingly light and healthy.
