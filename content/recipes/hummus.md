## Raw idea:

Tinned chickpeas, tahini, lemon, garlic, ice water — blitzed until impossibly smooth. The secret is more tahini than you think and blending longer than feels necessary. Serve with olive oil and paprika. Shortcut activation: store-bought hummus thinned with lemon juice and topped with good olive oil.
