## Overview
A Greek salad that eats like a creamy chopped salad: everything gets tossed hard so **Danish feta** breaks down and “melts” into the dressing, coating the veg.

## Raw idea

A Greek salad that eats like a creamy chopped salad: everything gets tossed hard so **Danish feta** breaks down and “melts” into the dressing, coating the veg. If you’ve got it, use a spoon of **mint-garlic-yoghurt** as the creamy base (not essential) and loosen with good olive oil and fresh lemon juice. Finish with dried oregano, salt, and plenty of black pepper — it should taste bright, salty, and punchy. It’s a perfect cooling side for spicy chicken or tacos, and it also cuts through rich **steak frites** beautifully.
