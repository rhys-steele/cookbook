## Raw Idea
A Greek salad that eats like a creamy chopped salad: everything gets tossed hard so Danish feta breaks down and 'melts' into the dressing, coating the veg. Use mint-garlic-yoghurt as the creamy base if you have it, loosen with olive oil and lemon juice. Finish with oregano, salt, and black pepper — bright, salty, punchy. Perfect cooling side for spicy chicken, tacos, or rich steak.

## Overview
A Greek salad that eats like a creamy chopped salad. The secret is aggressive tossing — Danish feta breaks down and 'melts' into the dressing, coating every vegetable in creamy, salty, tangy goodness.

## Why This Works
Danish feta is creamier and softer than traditional Greek feta. When tossed aggressively, it breaks apart and emulsifies with the yoghurt and olive oil, creating a creamy coating rather than staying in crumbles. The technique mimics a Caesar salad but with Greek flavors. The lemon juice adds acidity, the mint adds brightness, and the oregano ties it all together.

## Key Technique
**Aggressive tossing.** Don't be polite. You want to physically break down the feta so it becomes a dressing, not just chunks in a salad. 30-60 seconds of serious tossing transforms this from a standard Greek salad into something creamy and special.

## Shortcut
Use pre-crumbled feta and bottled lemon juice. Skip the garlic. Buy pre-cut vegetables if needed. It won't be quite as good, but still tasty.

## Variations
- **Classic Greek**: Skip the tossing technique, keep feta in large chunks, dress lightly with just oil and lemon
- **With protein**: Add grilled chicken, lamb, or prawns to make it a main
- **Kalamata olives**: Add 1/2 cup pitted olives for classic Greek flavor
- **Pickled onions**: Use quick-pickled red onions instead of raw for less bite

## Storage
- **Fridge**: Best eaten immediately. Will last 1 day in fridge but becomes watery as vegetables release moisture.
- **Freezer**: Does not freeze well
- **Counter**: Serve immediately - this is a fresh salad

## Make Ahead
You can prep all vegetables and keep them separate from dressing for up to 4 hours. Toss just before serving.

## Troubleshooting
- **Feta not breaking down?** You might have Greek feta (too firm) instead of Danish. Add more yoghurt and toss harder, or use your hands to crumble it more.
- **Too watery?** Cucumbers releasing moisture. Pat them dry with paper towels before adding, or salt them for 10 minutes and drain.
- **Not creamy enough?** Add more yoghurt or feta, and toss more aggressively.
