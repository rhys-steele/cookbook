## Overview
A baharat-ish blend — cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, allspice, black pepper, and a hint of clove. Toast whole spices and grind, or mix pre-ground. Use on roast vegetables, lamb, chicken, or stir into yoghurt. Shortcut is store-bought baharat or ras el hanout.
