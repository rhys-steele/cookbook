## Overview
Softened butter mixed with fresh parsley, chives, garlic, and lemon zest. Rolls into a log and freezes well. Slice off rounds for steaks, fish, vegetables, or toast. The shortcut is store-bought garlic butter activated with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
