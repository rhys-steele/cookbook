## Raw idea:

Shredded rotisserie chicken and chopped boiled eggs bound with mayo, dijon, and chives. Piled onto bread with cheese and grilled until melty. A diner-style comfort sandwich that uses up leftover chicken. Add pickled red onions for brightness and crunch.
