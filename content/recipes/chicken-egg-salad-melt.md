## Raw idea:

A diner-style comfort melt designed to use leftover **Nduja Yoghurt BBQ Chicken** (or rotisserie chicken as the shortcut). Shred the chicken and mix with chopped boiled eggs, mayo (kewpie if you’ve got it), and dijon, then fold through chopped pickles and jalapeños for crunch, heat, and acidity. Optional extras: a hit of hot sauce (Frank’s is perfect) and/or extra mustard depending on how punchy you want it. Pile onto toasted sourdough bread with cheese and grill until molten; finish with pickled red onions if you want even more brightness.
