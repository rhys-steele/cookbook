## Raw idea:

Made from a "stock bag" — freeze chicken bones, onion ends, carrot peels, celery leaves over time. When full, cover with water and simmer for hours. Strain and freeze in portions. Shortcut is good-quality store-bought stock reduced slightly to concentrate flavour.
