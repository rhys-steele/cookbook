## Raw Idea
Save chicken bones, vegetable scraps in freezer bag. When full, simmer with water for 3+ hours. Strain, freeze in portions. Free flavor from scraps you'd throw away. Shortcut is good store-bought stock reduced slightly.

## Overview
Made from a 'stock bag' — freeze chicken bones, onion ends, carrot peels, celery leaves over time. When full, cover with water and simmer for hours. Strain and freeze in portions. The foundation for soups, sauces, and risotto.

## Why This Works
Long, gentle simmering extracts gelatin from bones and connective tissue, creating body and mouthfeel. Vegetables add aromatics. Cold water start helps extract more flavor than hot water. Low temperature prevents cloudiness and bitter flavors.

## Key Technique
**Gentle simmer, not boil.** A rolling boil makes cloudy, bitter stock. You want gentle bubbles, barely breaking the surface. Patience extracts the good stuff without the bad.

## Shortcut
Buy good-quality chicken stock (check ingredients - should be chicken, vegetables, water, minimal additives). Reduce by 1/3 in a pot to concentrate flavor. Or use stock concentrate/bouillon - just choose brands with clean ingredients.

## Variations
- **Roasted bones**: Roast bones at 200°C for 30 mins before simmering for deeper, richer flavor
- **Pressure cooker**: 90 minutes at high pressure for same results as 4 hours simmering
- **Asian-style**: Add ginger, star anise, and omit carrots for pho-style stock

## Storage
- **Fridge**: 5 days in airtight container
- **Freezer**: 6 months - freeze in ice cube trays or 2-cup portions for easy use
- **Counter**: Not recommended

## Make Ahead
This IS a make-ahead staple. Batch cook and freeze in portions. Having homemade stock in the freezer unlocks easy weeknight cooking.

## Troubleshooting
- **Cloudy stock?** Boiled too hard. Strain through cheesecloth for clearer stock.
- **Doesn't gel when cold?** Not enough bones/cartilage. Add chicken feet or wings next time, or simmer longer.
- **Tastes weak?** Reduce it down by simmering uncovered to concentrate flavor.
- **Greasy?** Chill and remove the fat cap, or skim while hot.

## Uses / Pairs With
Chicken Pho, Risotto, Soups, Pan sauces, Braising liquid

## Additional Notes
Don't salt the stock - season the final dish instead, giving you control over salt levels. Vegetable scraps are great (onion skins add color) but avoid cabbage family (too strong) and starchy vegetables (makes stock cloudy). This is truly zero-waste cooking - trash becomes treasure.
