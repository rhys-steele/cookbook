## Raw idea:

Slow-cooked beef in a rich, spicy birria-style sauce until it collapses and pulls apart easily, with a side of reduced consommé for dipping (the “wet taco” moment is the hook). Build tacos in warm tortillas and go hard on acidity and crunch: fresh coriander/cilantro, lime, and **pickled onions**. The twist is re-using the **mint-garlic-yoghurt** as the cooling element — either as a drizzle inside the taco or as a side dip to balance the heat and richness (thin it with lime/salt if you want it more sauce-like). Comforting, messy, perfect winter food.
