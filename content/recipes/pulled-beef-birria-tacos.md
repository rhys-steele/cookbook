## Raw idea:

Slow-cooked beef in a rich, spicy birria sauce until tender enough to pull apart. Serve the pulled beef in warm corn tortillas, topped with fresh cilantro, pickled onions, and a squeeze of lime. Accompany with a side of consommé for dipping the tacos, enhancing the flavors and adding moisture. The combination of tender beef, spicy sauce, and fresh toppings creates a comforting and satisfying meal perfect for colder months.
