## Overview
Wedges of kent pumpkin tossed in olive oil and warm spice mix, roasted until caramelised and tender. Served on a platter over mint garlic yoghurt, scattered with pomegranate seeds, toasted pepitas, and fresh herbs. Impressive centrepiece that's actually simple. Great with flatbread for scooping.
